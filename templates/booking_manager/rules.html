Rules of Attendance

To ensure the highest quality of service and a comfortable atmosphere for everyone, please follow our simple guidelines.

  1. Punctuality: Please arrive 5–10 minutes before your scheduled appointment. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your visit.
  2. Cancellations: If you need to cancel or change the time of your booking, please notify us at least 2 hours in advance.
  3. Health & Safety: For the safety of our masters and clients, please refrain from visiting the salon if you show symptoms of acute illness or fever.
  4. Children: Children under 12 years old must be accompanied by an adult. Please ensure they remain seated for their own safety around sharp tools.
  5. Atmosphere: We maintain a relaxed and friendly vibe. Please switch your devices to silent mode and respect the comfort of other guests.
